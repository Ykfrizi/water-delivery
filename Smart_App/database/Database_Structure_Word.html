1. Overview

The schema separates identity (users, Sanctum personal_access_tokens), vendor storefront data (vendor_profiles, delivery_zones, products), and social proof (reviews). Laravel queue/cache/session tables support framework features.

  • users.role distinguishes customer, vendor, and admin accounts (application enum).
  • vendor_profiles.user_id links each vendor storefront to exactly one user.
  • reviews tie a customer user to a vendor profile with a uniqueness constraint on (customer_id, vendor_profile_id).

12. Relationship summary

  • users 1 — 0..1 vendor_profiles (vendors only).
  • vendor_profiles 1 — * delivery_zones, products, reviews.
  • users (customers) 1 — * reviews as customer_id.
  • users 1 — * personal_access_tokens (polymorphic).
